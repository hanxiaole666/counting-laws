--- BEGIN HIGHLIGHTS ---
The global-key budget, not sparsity, decides whether attention is linear in n.
Periodic anchors use 669x the memory of a fused kernel at n=32768 on 6 GiB.
Block kernels run min(1,B/g) density, inflating advertised sparsity up to 98x.
A yes/no logit interface costs 22x less than parsed JSON and cannot fail.
LoRA on 900 rows fixes the output format and pushes AUC-ROC 0.64 to chance.
